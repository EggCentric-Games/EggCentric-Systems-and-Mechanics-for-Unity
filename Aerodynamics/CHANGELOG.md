## [0.0.1] - 2025-08-01

- Implemented aerodynamic surfaces
- Implemented deflectable aerodynamic surfaces
- Implemented composite aerodynamic surfaces
- Implemented simple procedural geometry for wings

- Implemented two separate strategies for low and high AoA-s (pre-stall and post-stall accordingly)
- Implemented smooth blending between pre-stall and post-stall behaviours

- Created Samples~ folder for future use
